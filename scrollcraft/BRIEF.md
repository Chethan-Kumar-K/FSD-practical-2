# CarbonFootprint India: Carbon Receipt Story

**Creative authority:** Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. The user asked for an insane, gradient-rich landing experience using Scroll Craft extensively while preserving every existing feature and pushing to the connected GitHub repository.

## Design read

- **Artifact:** Product-story landing layer integrated into a working carbon-footprint dashboard.
- **Audience:** People who want to understand the carbon cost of daily travel, energy, food, and shopping choices, then log an activity and change one lever.
- **Visual language:** Climate editorial meets live data instrument. Deep forest surfaces, warm receipt paper, oxidized gold, mineral teal, contour lines, and purposeful gradients.
- **Mode:** Redesign · Preserve. Existing CRUD, analytics, theme, navigation, reminders, trip tracking, pledges, import/export, and accessibility contracts remain intact.
- **Visual variance:** 9/10.
- **Motion intensity:** 9/10, reduced to a calm readable flow when requested by the user’s system.
- **Information density:** 7/10. The story is atmospheric, but every number comes from the live analysis state.
- **Asset dependence:** 2/10. No external photography is required. The project’s real receipt and data are the visual material.
- **Brand fidelity:** 8/10 to the existing CarbonFootprint India palette and typography.

## Audience and purpose

This is a working carbon-footprint tracker. The landing layer should make the product’s promise felt immediately: ordinary choices become a receipt, the receipt reveals the biggest lever, and the user can act without leaving the page.

## Journey beats

1. **Recognition:** The visitor sees a carbon receipt for their actual day, not a fabricated hero statistic.
2. **Tension:** Scroll separates the receipt into categories and makes the largest current contributor visually impossible to miss.
3. **Turn:** The receipt stops being a judgement and becomes a control surface. The live analysis shows where a small change can matter.
4. **Substance:** Category cards and the real dashboard summary explain the calculation without hiding the empty state.
5. **Range:** A horizontal category rail turns travel, home energy, food, and shopping into four practical levers.
6. **Commitment:** The story hands off to the existing activity form, where the visitor can log the next choice.

## Feeling curve

- **Recognition:** Curious and personally addressed. The receipt and layered atmosphere make the visitor’s own data feel tangible.
- **Tension:** Focused, not guilty. Pinning and category isolation name the largest live line item.
- **Turn:** Energised. The receipt transforms from paper into a responsive data instrument.
- **Substance:** Grounded. The actual summary and category totals prove this is a working tool.
- **Range:** Expansive. Horizontal travel across the four real catalogue categories makes action feel available.
- **Commitment:** Clear and capable. The form is the next move, not a dead-end CTA.

## Peak

> “It just turned my everyday choices into a receipt I can actually change.”

The peak lives in the pinned receipt transformation, where the live total and largest category become a bright, spatial data mark before resolving into the real dashboard summary.

## Chosen page grammar

**Live surface hybrid:** a working product surface is the destination, while the opening chapters behave like a short editorial instrument. This fits better than a filmic one-shot because the visitor must eventually use the tracker, inspect their actual data, and retain access to all existing controls. Distinct scenes, a pinned transformation, and a horizontal category rail create variation without hiding the app.

## Device score

| Beat | Device | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Recognition | Layered hero parallax | Gives receipt paper, atmosphere, contours, and data marks independent depth.
| Tension | Pinned narrative/state progression | Holds attention while the current analysis moves through a focused sequence.
| Turn | Scrubbed receipt transformation | Makes the signature move track the visitor’s hand exactly.
| Substance | Staggered live-data reveal | Lets authentic summary values arrive as evidence rather than decoration.
| Range | Horizontal category rail | Lateral movement reads as a set of choices and keeps the page from feeling like repeated cards.
| Commitment | Scroll-to-product handoff | The existing form remains a real, immediate action surface.

No device family is repeated consecutively. The page does not use video, fabricated counters, or a generic purple/pink tech gradient.

## Signature move

**The carbon receipt becomes the map.** As the visitor scrubs through the pinned story, the receipt’s paper plane rotates and opens into a vertical carbon spectrum. Its live total, largest category, and category bars recompose from the current analysis data. The same values then reappear in the real dashboard summary, making the transition from story to product state feel continuous rather than promotional.

## Gradient and token strategy

Gradients are climate/data semantics, not decoration: deep forest is the canvas, mineral teal indicates the living system, oxidized gold marks attention and budget, warm paper carries the receipt, and restrained cyan marks data movement. Radial gradients create atmospheric depth, linear gradients encode category transitions, and contour/grid layers provide spatial orientation. No gradient text is used.

## Mobile and reduced-motion strategy

On mobile, the hero becomes a stacked receipt-first composition with shorter parallax distances. The pinned story remains readable as a compact flow sequence; the category rail becomes a native horizontal scroll region with all cards reachable. Reduced motion disables scrubbing, pinning, rotations, and entrance choreography while keeping every heading, value, category, and form in normal document flow. Decorative layers are `aria-hidden`; real headings, links, buttons, and data remain semantic.

## Authored silence

A short quiet hold appears between the tension copy and the receipt transformation. It is intentional: the visitor should see the largest live line before the page accelerates into the peak.

## Tell-someone sentence

It’s the site where your everyday choices print themselves into a carbon receipt, then show you the one line worth changing next.
